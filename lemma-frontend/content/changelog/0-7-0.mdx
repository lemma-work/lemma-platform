## Slack as a native surface

Agents stream their replies into Slack as they think, App Home carries setup,
and a pod can be configured without leaving the workspace.

## Content and documentation

Docs, the blog and the changelog now render from MDX with a shared component
vocabulary, syntax highlighting that follows your appearance, and structured
data on every public page.

## Fixes

- Share links no longer reflect provider errors back to the page
- Callback pages share one design across every provider

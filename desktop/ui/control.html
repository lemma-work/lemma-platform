Signed release lifecycle

Installing a newer signed Lemma app stages its exact matching runtime on the next local launch. The old verified pack is retained, but schema-1 releases do not claim database-safe downgrade compatibility.

Rollback is intentionally unavailable until a release declares its data rollback boundary. Lemma will not risk opening migrated data with an older backend.

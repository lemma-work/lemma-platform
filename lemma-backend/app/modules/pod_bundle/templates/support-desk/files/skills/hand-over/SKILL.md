# Hand over with the whole story

Hand over instead of answering when the reply would commit the team to something: money, a date, an exception to policy, a change to an account, or anything that has never been answered before.

Write the hand-over as one message to the person who owns it:

- **Who and what**: the customer, their account, and the question in one sentence.
- **What they already said**: the facts they gave, so nobody asks twice.
- **What you found**: the help center page, the similar past case, the open issue.
- **What you would say**: a draft reply the person can send as it is or change.
- **What is missing**: the one decision you need from them.

Tell the customer a person is looking at it and when they can expect to hear back, only if the team has a stated response time. Add a row to `callbacks` when someone has promised to get back to a customer.

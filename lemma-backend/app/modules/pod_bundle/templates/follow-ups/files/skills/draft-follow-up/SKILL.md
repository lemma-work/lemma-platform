# Draft a follow-up

1. Read the commitment and the conversation it came from. Say what was promised, in the owner's voice.
2. Keep it short: what you are following up on, what you need or what you are sending, and one clear next step.
3. Draft it for the owner to send. Do not send it yourself unless the owner has said follow-ups may go out without them.
4. Set `followed_up_at` when the follow-up goes out, not when it is drafted.

If the commitment is more than a week late, say so plainly in the draft and offer a new date rather than apologising at length.
